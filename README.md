# Dilan Encuentros Conscientes

Design a demo website for Dra. Gretchelle Dilán, Ph.D., a psychologist and sexologist specializing in couples therapy. Her current site is https://www.psicoanalizar.com (7 years old). Build a brand new design from scratch, not a copy of it. All website content in Spanish.

STYLE
Editorial "writer / author" style: elegant, warm and calm, like the website of a published author. Classic serif headings (like Playfair Display or Cormorant), clean sans serif body text, generous white space, soft neutral palette (ivory, warm beige, deep charcoal, one muted accent such as terracotta or sage). Large portrait photos, magazine style layouts, pull quotes. Fully responsive.

SITE PLAN (menu)
Inicio · Sobre mí · Terapias · Citas · Cursos · Contacto

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS
1. Hero: large portrait of Dra. Dilán, headline "Terapia de pareja con enfoque humano y científico", short subtext, buttons "Agendar cita" and "Conocer más".
2. Credentials strip: Doctorado en Psicología · Post grado en Sexualidad, Universidad de Barcelona · Certificación Gottman nivel 3 · Licenciada por la Junta Examinadora de Psicólogos de PR · Mediadora certificada, Tribunal General de Justicia de PR · 17 años de consulta en línea.
3. About intro: editorial two column layout with photo and short bio (Ponce, Puerto Rico, online sessions; university professor, researcher, mediator). Link to Sobre mí.
4. Approach: "Terapia cognitivo conductual y terapias contemporáneas con bases constructivistas", shown as an elegant pull quote.
5. Therapies: cards for Terapia de pareja, Terapia individual, Infidelidad, Dependencia emocional, Relaciones tóxicas, Separación y divorcio, Ansiedad y depresión por vínculos amorosos.
6. How it works: 3 steps (Agenda tu cita · Primera sesión en línea · Plan terapéutico).
7. Booking section: online appointment booking card with session type, date and time, plus a WhatsApp button (+1 787 409 1516).
8. Courses: "Próximamente" section with an email signup to be notified when courses launch.
9. Testimonials placeholder (3 anonymous quotes).
10. Footer: contact, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok (@dragretchelledilan), and a confidentiality note.

OTHER PAGES (simple layouts)
Sobre mí: full bio and credentials timeline.
Terapias: detail for each therapy type.
Citas: full booking page.
Cursos: coming soon page with signup.
Contacto: form, WhatsApp and social links.

Use elegant placeholder images of a professional woman therapist in a calm office until her real photos are added.

Also attached: real photos of Dra. Dilán (photographed from a screen, some are blurry or show screen edges, so crop/clean them as needed) and her circular logo badge. Use the real photos where quality allows in place of placeholders, and the badge as the logo mark.
